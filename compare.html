How Bored Scanner compares

Checked October 29, 2026. Prices are US list prices before tax, from each vendor's own site or App Store listing. They change; check before you buy.

Most asset tools are platforms: they want to be where your inventory lives, and they're priced that way. Bored Scanner isn't one. It's a 99¢ way to get labels into the system you already have, or into a spreadsheet if you don't have one yet.

Being fair about it

  • Others read labels too. Scandit Express and S/N Scanner also combine barcode and text recognition, and Scandit documents MDM rollout. Bored Scanner's difference is the price and the scope: one job, 99¢, straight into Snipe-IT or a spreadsheet.
  • Every Snipe-IT app needs a Snipe-IT server, including ours. Self-hosted Snipe-IT is free software, but a server, updates and someone to look after it are not. Hosted Snipe-IT starts at $39.99/month. That's a separate bill from any of the apps above.
  • Platforms do more. Reftab, Sortly, Asset Panda and Scandit's partners offer reporting, workflows and integrations a 99¢ app doesn't. If you need those, you need a platform.
  • We haven't benchmarked these apps hands-on. This table comes from each vendor's published pages, not from scanning the same labels side by side.
  • "Enterprise?" describes how each product is offered and sold. It isn't a security or compliance rating.
